Signal Relay Chain

BETA TESTING

A squad-coordination objective, not a puzzle. Zeus syncs a set of relay props in the order they should activate - only the current relay lights up, and someone has to physically hold position near it to channel it before the chain advances to the next.

  1. Zeus places one Signal Relay Chain module and syncs it to relay props (or any objects) in activation order.
  2. Only the current relay in the sequence is lit - the rest stay dark until their turn.
  3. A player stands near the active relay to channel it - leaving pauses progress rather than resetting it.
  4. Once channelled, that relay goes dark and the next one in the chain lights up elsewhere.

Note — no item or tool required - this is pure positioning and time under pressure, spread across multiple locations rather than one static console.
